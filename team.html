Team & Lab

COLOSSEO is a collaboratorium: faculty, postdocs, PhD and master's students from Earth sciences and computer science at Sapienza and other institutions, working with INGV and colleagues abroad. Over five years the project will support 4 postdocs, 6 PhD students and a laboratory specialist.

The laboratory

Experiments run in the Rock Mechanics and Earthquake Physics Laboratory of the Dipartimento di Scienze della Terra. Its servo-hydraulic machines reproduce the full spectrum of fault slip, from aseismic creep to slow slip and fast lab earthquakes, while recording the acoustic emissions and elastic-wave changes that AI models learn from.

  • Large biaxial apparatus · faults up to 80 cm
  • Biaxial / true-triaxial with pressure vessel
  • Forces to 1 MN
  • Confining pressure to 100 MPa
  • Active + passive ultrasonics, 1 pulse/ms
  • Eddy-current slip sensors, sub-micron
  • Digital image correlation
